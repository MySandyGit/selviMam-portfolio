CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about therapeutic style, confidentiality, online effectiveness, and what to expect in session.

How does online therapy compare to in-person sessions?
Clinical studies and client experience demonstrate that online psychotherapy delivers comparable therapeutic depth and outcomes for most clients when conducted in a quiet, confidential space. Many clients find it easier to integrate somatic awareness into their everyday home environment.
How long will I need to be in therapy?
Therapy duration is collaborative and customized. Some individuals seek short-term counseling (8 to 14 sessions) focused on a specific life transition, while others choose open-ended psychodynamic exploration to address deeply rooted emotional patterns. We periodically review our progress together.
How is client confidentiality maintained?
Everything shared in session is held under strict professional confidentiality in accordance with clinical psychology ethics. Information is never disclosed to family members, employers, or third parties except in rare cases where there is an imminent risk of serious physical harm to yourself or others.
What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?
We maintain a 24-hour notice policy for cancellations and rescheduling. Because your session time is reserved solely for you, cancellations within 24 hours are charged at the standard rate, except in sudden medical emergencies.
